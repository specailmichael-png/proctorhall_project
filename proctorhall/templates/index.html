How a session runs

01

Professor builds the exam

Set a title, duration and multiple-choice questions from the admin dashboard. Publish when ready.

02

Student takes it, camera on

The browser asks for camera access, checks for a single face, and enforces full-screen for the duration.

03

Violations are logged, not guessed

Every flagged moment — a second face, a phone, a tab switch — lands in a timestamped log the professor can review.

What it checks for

Face presence

Flags when no face, or more than one face, is visible in frame.

Gaze & head pose

Estimates whether the candidate is looking at the screen or consistently away from it.

Object detection

Watches for phones or other unauthorised devices entering the frame.

Tab & window focus

Detects when the exam tab loses focus or the browser leaves full-screen.

Timed sessions

Auto-submits when time runs out, no extensions needed.

Reviewable logs

Professors see a per-student violation timeline, not just a final score.
